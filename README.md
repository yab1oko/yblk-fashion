# YBLK Fashion

**YBLK Fashion** is wearable clothing for adventure maps and survival servers - the kind of gear you actually put on a character, not a reskin of vanilla armor.

You get everyday wear (shirts, hoodies, coats, hats, shoes) next to scavenged scrap plating, face coverings, glasses, a gas mask, and NVG. Clothes sit in normal armor slots or Curios (outerwear, vest, pants, mask, bags), and the models keep a proper cut against the player skin so sleeves and hems don't leave empty gaps.

Most pieces are made at the Sewing Station: drop in fabric and thread, pick a dye when you want color, and preview the result on a mannequin before you take it. Some garments accept small mods - a pouch, an armor plate, a cigarette on a hat - without turning the whole outfit into a separate item.

<img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_e1ba7cd3-75f5-400d-8d2c-da0f39e03f3a.png" alt="" width="520">
</p>

Optionally pairs with [YBLK Features](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yblk-features) (extra inventory slots while clothes are worn) and [Roleplay Materials](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/roleplay-materials) (gas filters, NVG batteries, scrap repair plates).
