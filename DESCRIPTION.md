## ✨ Overview
YBLK Fashion adds clothing you actually put on a character — not a reskin of vanilla armor. It is built for adventure maps and survival servers that want scavenged outfits, masks, and layered gear. [Curios API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) is required so clothes can sit in armor slots or Curios slots (outerwear, vest, pants, mask, bags).

## 👕 Features
Everyday wear covers shirts, hoodies, coats, hats, and shoes. Scavenged gear adds scrap plating, face coverings, glasses, a gas mask, and NVG. Models keep a proper cut against the player skin so sleeves and hems do not leave empty gaps.

Most pieces are made at the Sewing Station: drop in fabric and thread, pick a dye when you want color, and preview the result on a mannequin before you take it. Some garments accept small additions — a pouch, an armor plate, a cigarette on a hat — without turning the outfit into a separate item.

Optionally pairs with [YBLK Features](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yblk-features) for extra inventory slots while clothes are worn, and [Roleplay Materials](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/roleplay-materials) for gas filters, NVG batteries, and scrap repair plates.

## 🎮 How to use
Craft a Sewing Station, drop in fabric and thread, add a dye if you want color, check the mannequin preview, then take the garment. Wear it in an armor slot or a Curios slot. Apply small mods (pouch, plate, and similar) to compatible garments when you want them.
